## Project Overview
This lab focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using the Titanic dataset. The goal was to examine the dataset before model building by using summary statistics and visualizations to identify patterns, relationships, missing values, and unusual observations.

We explored how different passenger characteristics were related to survival, including passenger class, gender, age, fare, and port of embarkation.

## Main Tasks
- Loaded and examined the Titanic dataset
- Reviewed descriptive statistics and missing values
- Created visualizations to explore survival patterns
- Compared survival by passenger class and gender
- Analyzed age distribution by survival
- Completed an experiment on port of embarkation and survival
- Completed an experiment on fare and survival
- Answered the Knowledge Check questions
- Completed a reflection journal and individual contribution journal

## Key Findings
The analysis showed that passenger class and gender had strong relationships with survival. First-class passengers and females had higher survival rates. Young children also appeared to have a better chance of survival.

The experimentation section also showed that passengers who boarded at Cherbourg had a different survival pattern from those who boarded at Southampton and Queenstown. Passengers who survived generally paid higher fares as well. However, these relationships may also connect to other factors such as passenger class, so they should not automatically be treated as direct causes of survival.
